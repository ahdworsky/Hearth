Privacy Policy

This is a private household application ("DWORSKY") used by one family to coordinate a shared calendar, chores, meal planning, and lists. It is not a public product or commercial service.

Information we collect

Phone numbers and email addresses are collected only if a family member chooses to enter them, for the sole purpose of sending household reminders (such as chore due dates).

How we use it

Phone numbers are used only to send SMS reminders about household chores. Email addresses are used only to send email reminders about household chores. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies based on chores assigned, typically no more than once per day.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share phone numbers or email addresses with any third party for marketing or any other purpose. Numbers are used solely for the household reminder purpose described above.

Opting out

Reply STOP to any text message to stop receiving SMS reminders, or contact the household administrator directly to be removed at any time.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the household administrator.
